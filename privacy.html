Privacy & Diagnostic Terms

Effective 31 July 2026. RestroRx is operated by Chef Amey Marathe as a restaurant diagnostic and consulting service.

What we collect

We collect the contact, restaurant, operational, and financial estimates you choose to submit, along with the diagnosis generated from those inputs. Please do not submit payment-card details, government IDs, employee personal data, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Why we use it

We use the information to produce your restaurant diagnosis, review your situation, contact you about the result or a consultation, secure the service, and improve the RestroRx framework. We do not sell your personal information.

Storage and service providers

Information is processed through service providers used to host the website and store lead records. Access is restricted to Chef Amey and authorised operators. Diagnostic inputs may be processed by an automated analysis provider when available; the core RestroRx rules also run without it.

Retention and your choices

Records are retained only while useful for follow-up, consulting, security, or legal obligations. You can ask for access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal from follow-up by messaging the RestroRx WhatsApp number linked on the main site.

Diagnostic limitation

RestroRx provides an initial operational estimate based on information you supply. It is not an audit, accounting opinion, legal advice, or a guarantee of profit. Validate major decisions against your books and qualified advisers.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Chef Amey through WhatsApp.
